# Naming

The brand is **Sovereign Intelligence (SI)**. "Sovereign Intelligence" and "SI" are names I claim as my
trademarks (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). This page says how to write them.

## The rules

1. **Always capitalized.** Sovereign Intelligence, never "sovereign intelligence" when it means this project.
2. **Spell it out on first use.** Write "Sovereign Intelligence (SI)" the first time, then "SI".
3. **Mark the first prominent mention.** In headlines, heroes and release titles, the first
   "Sovereign Intelligence" carries ™: Sovereign Intelligence™. Later mentions do not.
4. **Spell it out wherever science or units appear.** "SI" also means the International System of Units.
   On any page with physics, chemistry, math results or units, write "Sovereign Intelligence" in full
   every time, so "SI" can only ever mean units there.
5. **Say what it is built on.** Write "Sovereign Intelligence, built on AienOS".
   AienOS keeps its own name and its own home in [aien-dev/AienOS-nv](https://github.com/aien-dev/AienOS-nv).
   SI never replaces or absorbs the AienOS name.
6. **Keep the other names as they are.** Aienux is the kernel ([aien-dev/aienux](https://github.com/aien-dev/aienux)).
   Omega, CUDA++ and Aien are parts of SI, not other names for it. Aien is the assistant's name
   (capital A, lowercase ien). Allen has now evolved into the final Aien (2026-10-26); his
   repository moved from `allen` to [aien-dev/aien](https://github.com/aien-dev/aien) the same day.

my step to take as the owner; no agent and nothing in this repository files or registers anything.
Until then the ™ claim in [NOTICE](NOTICE) is the whole of it.
